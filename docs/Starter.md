## What Ariane does

Ariane is a cave surveying and mapping application. It takes raw survey shots — measured with a
compass and a depth gauge, or recorded by a dedicated instrument such as the
[MNemo](https://manuals.arianesline.com/mnemo/) or the
[JedEye](https://manuals.arianesline.com/jedeye/) — and turns them into:

- a **corrected 3D cave network**, with loop closure by three different adjustment algorithms;
- a **drawn 2D map**, with layers, styles, overlays and page setup for printing;
- **elevation profiles**, either simple LRUD or full vectorial cross-sections;
- **statistics and reports** on length, depth, sections, surveyors and explorers;
- **exports** to SVG, KML, OBJ, Compass MAK, CSV, animated GIF and HTML.
